## Recording notes

- Beat 1 needs the autoplay from a fresh load; reload between takes so the 74-min figure stays hidden until the playhead passes 14:26.
- Beat 3: let the first few cards land before opening one, so the ms figures are visible. At the default clock the path and fix cards are memoized and say "cached" next to the time. Show it; do not crop it out.
- Beats 4 and 5 use mocha because its path is zero hops and its pinned version is visibly safe. `github/docs` (`chalk ^5.0.1`, pins 5.4.1) is the backup if mocha's row is off screen.
- Beat 6 must run on the qix scenario (the 14 repos / 6 teams figure is for qix at 13:30), which is why Ask comes before the axios switch.
- Beat 8: pick a public repo with a `package-lock.json` and warm it beforehand so the fetch is quick on camera. Cut the wait if GitHub is slow.
- The voiceover is about 330 words, roughly 2:10 of speech at a measured pace, which leaves room for pauses on the clicks. If a beat runs long, trim the voiceover, not the on-screen action.

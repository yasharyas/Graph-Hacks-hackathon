# Patient Zero: Phase 3 round 5, keyboard and screen-reader QA

Persona: keyboard-only screen-reader user (`keyboard.press` only for navigation and activation), judged against WCAG 2.2 AA: visible focus, logical order, no traps, focus not obscured, sensible live regions, roles/names/states, reduced motion, forced colors, reflow at 400%.

Method: headless Chromium via Playwright (`playwright-skill` install). Production build `localhost:8080` for everything (axe, announcements, walkthrough, zoom), Vite dev `localhost:5173` re-run for axe (all 26 states), announcement counts, the skip-link sweep and autoplay/slider checks (identical results; the only dev-only difference is the usual motion-library warning). Viewports 1440x900, 375x812, 320x256 as asked, plus 1920x1080, 1536x864, 1366x768, 1280x720, 1280x800, 1180x820, 1100x700, 1024x768, 900x700, 768x1024 and 720x450 (200% zoom) for the focus-visibility sweep. axe-core (repo copy) injected in every state, all rules plus the wcag2a/2aa/21a/21aa/22aa + best-practice tag set; on :8080 the CSP blocks injection, so only the axe runs (and the text-spacing style injection) used Playwright `bypassCSP`; every other prod check ran without it. A MutationObserver recorder on `[aria-live]` / `role=status|alert|log` flags regions **inserted already holding text** (`INSERTED-WITH-CONTENT`) and, new this round, **ignores regions that are not rendered** (display:none, `inert`, `aria-hidden`), because a screen reader never speaks those. One pass each with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and `forced-colors: active`. No repo file edited, no commits, `.env` not read. Other QA agents shared the servers, so absolute timings are approximate; counts and focus facts are exact. Screenshots: `phase3r5-a11y/` (95 files).

Limits: no real NVDA/JAWS/VoiceOver. "Announced" means "a polite/assertive live region changed text".

## 1. Verdict

**All of round 4's blocking and nice-to-have items are fixed or explained (details in section 3). A deeper focus-visibility sweep, which I should have done in round 4, found three scroll-and-focus defects that are real WCAG 2.4.7 / 2.4.11 failures in common situations. Overall 8.8/10 (round 4: 8.8). Accessibility stays at 8: the blockers are all one family (the "bring the focused thing into view" logic) and all small.**

What got better, in one breath: the dimmed narrative and the remediated row now pass contrast (no opacity on the stale narrative any more, min 4.94:1 in 12 states, the done row is about 6.7:1), the landing page reflows at 320x256 (scrollWidth 320/320 in nine states), focusing the slider pauses autoplay, the guest re-check keeps focus, a triage is **3 announcements** on prod (was 8 to 10), the first scrub status is a real change rather than an insert, Esc/Enter on a toast's Dismiss returns focus, the run-in label spacing and the `@tapjs/run` name are fixed, filtering says one thing per word typed and sorting says "Sorted by repo, ascending". axe is clean in 25 of 26 states on both builds (the 26th is the known forced-colors false positive), and also clean at 320x256 and 1024x768.

What is left, all in the focus-into-view logic (`ToolFeed.tsx` `toCalls`, `scrollPane.ts` `reveal`/`scrollerOf`): **(1)** "Skip to tool calls" + Enter leaves the focused card header hidden above the visible area at every viewport I tried (24 px above the pane's top edge at 1280 and wider, entirely above the window at 1024 and narrower); **(2)** in the 1024 to 1279 px band the scroll logic works against a pane that is taller than the window, so "Show all N rows" and "Skip to Ask" can leave focus 300 to 500 px below the fold; **(3)** at 200% and 400% zoom the Ask input ends up under the pinned Panels bar (720x450) or 330 px below the fold (320x256) after an answer arrives. Plus five small nice-to-haves (scenario arrows restart a paused autoplay and make it chatty, 375 first-scrub status lives in a hidden pane, and so on).

Corrections to my own round 4: (a) I wrote that "Skip to tool calls" lands "in view". That check used viewport coordinates only; round 4's own screenshot `r4-06` shows the header already half cut by the pane top, and it is now fully hidden. This is a miss of mine, not a regression of this round. (b) I counted the guest re-check's "Step n of 5" lines as duplicate announcements. They live in a closed dialog (display:none) and are never spoken, so that was a recorder artifact; with the corrected recorder the re-check speaks one status per step.

## 6. Ranked fixes to reach >= 9 everywhere

1. **blocking-9 (Accessibility): "Skip to tool calls" leaves the focused card header hidden, at every viewport.** Repro: 1440x900, Tab, Tab, Enter (pause), Tab x8, Enter (triage); focus is on "Tool feed"; Tab (Skip to tool calls), Enter. Saw: focus is on `suspect_versions · qix` but its rect is y 103 to 125 while the feed pane starts at 126, so only a ring fragment shows (`r5-06`); at 1280x720 the same; at 1024x768, 768x1024, 720x450, 375x812 and 320x256 it is y -23 to -1, **outside the window** (`r5-155`). Cause (measured at 375): the skip nav is in the flow while focused (height 36 px); `reveal(article, 'top')` runs, then `target.focus()` blurs the link, the nav collapses to 0 height, and everything below moves up 36 px (article top 541 to -36). Expected: the header visible below the pane top / window top. Fix: move focus first, then `reveal` in a `requestAnimationFrame`, or take the skip nav out of flow (`position:absolute` when focused). WCAG 2.4.7, 2.4.11, 2.4.3. Suspected: `web/src/components/ToolFeed.tsx` `toCalls` (lines 170-176) and the `skip` class (line 296); `web/src/components/scrollPane.ts` `reveal`.
2. **blocking-9 (Accessibility): in the 1024 to 1279 px band, scroll-into-view works against a pane taller than the window.** Repro: 1024x768 (also 1100x700, 1180x820), run a triage, focus "Show all 19 rows" in the first card, Enter: focus ends on "Show the first 5 rows" at y 1070 to 1094 on a 768 px window (`r5-156`). Or: Expand all (or "Show 18 more"), focus the feed region, Tab to "Skip to Ask", Enter: the input is at y 1289 to 1325 (`r5-157`); after an answer it is only 72% visible.

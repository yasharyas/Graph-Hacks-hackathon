## Verdict

Round 5's three before-the-demo items are gone, and gone cleanly. **The repo column never cuts a name again**: the owner gives way (`ht…/http-server`, `ni…/nightwatch`, drawn from `data-drawn`) while the page text, `title` and accessible name stay whole, and 20 px moved from Path to Repo so 16 of 21 ids are whole at 1440 (round 5: 13). **The 1280x720 and 1024x768 laptops are one screen**: a `tight` room drops the Why sentence and the first FalkorDB card sits 38 px (1280) and 14 px (1024) above the Ask bar, and the 1024 page no longer scrolls 3 181 px. **"Run again" says what it discards** ("4 follow-ups from the 13:30 UTC run cleared: this run starts a fresh set of 4."). Five of the nice-to-haves landed too: the 12 px code floor is universal, the band's label is one line, Export is icon-only below 1366 on a one-row toolbar, phone tier chips have padding, the 1024 layout has internal scrollers. The round-6 additions are the right ones for a judge: the Fix tab opens on what is true today and, for a pasted repo, on a green "Checked against today's package.json: its direct dependencies still match this fix."; every team in the Notify roster has a working Copy notice (clipboard + toast); a yarn or pnpm repo gets a one-line reason, the exact command with Copy and a "How to check it by hand" disclosure, sticky under the fields. Fonts are Satoshi, Cabinet Grotesk and Martian Mono only; the new metric-matched mono fallback draws only inside a delayed swap window and the real face renders the same 272 glyphs once loaded (CLS 0.003). axe: 0 violations in seven states; contrast: 0 failures in 20 states; detector: 0 CLI findings and one real browser finding (a 94-character purpose line over the 68ch measure).

Two things the density round introduced are worth an hour before the demo. **A toast that is alive when a row is clicked jumps to the top-right, over the band's Run again button and the 14:26 / 15:15 labels**: a row click focuses the whole `Exposure results` pane, and the "never cover the focused element" rule in `Toasts.tsx` treats a 518x743 pane as covered (`04-row-selected-path.png`; Export and Copy notice toasts land bottom-right otherwise). **The Notify roster is prose**: seven teams, semicolon-joined, with a 12 px ghost "Copy notice" wedged between each team's name and its channel; on a phone it is a 20-line wall of 24 px links (`08e-notify-roster.png`, `m-08e-notify.png`). Assessment A, scoring fresh and unanchored, also makes a fair case that the tight room threw out the wrong sentence: the band, the verdict and the exec strip each say "21 of 21 · no advisory yet · 74 min" on the first screen, while the one path sentence (`WHY acme/checkout-web locks debug@4.4.2 through express@5.1.0 [E1]`) is behind a disclosure at 1280 and 1024; swapping the verdict's hindsight clause (≈ 50 px) for the Why (55 px) at `tight` costs no height. The rest is the carried P3 tail (two legends, the dotted underline, the schema run-on, the phone tab strip and "?", the alert autoplay, roving tabindex, spec drift) plus one regression: the axios `…/apidom-reference` path now loses 16 px, not one, after the column move. **Ship-ready for the judging bar; fix the toast rule and the roster, and give the tight room its Why.**

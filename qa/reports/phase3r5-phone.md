# Patient Zero: Phase 3 round 5, phone QA (LinkedIn/X-link persona)

Target: production build `http://localhost:8080/` (the link people tap). Vite dev `http://localhost:5173/` was only used for one throttled cold load. Chromium headless via Playwright, iPhone UA, `isMobile`, `hasTouch`, DPR 3, `tap()` everywhere, CDP touch for the scroll-inside-popover checks. Viewports: 375x812 (main), 390x844, 320x640, 812x375. Every flow ran in a fresh browser context (fresh session). Shots: `qa/reports/phase3r5-phone/` (file names in brackets). Scripts: scratchpad `r5phone/` (not in the repo). No repo file was edited.

Caveats, stated up front:
- Headless Chromium has no soft keyboard. "Keyboard up" is simulated by shrinking the layout viewport (Android resizes-content model). iOS and Chrome-Android visual-viewport behaviour is untested. WebKit was not available, so anything iOS-specific is reasoned, not observed.
- CLS: I report the standard value (shifts within 500 ms of a tap are excluded by the browser) and the raw total, because on this app the tap itself triggers a scroll jump plus page growth.
- axe-core was not re-run this round (round 4: 0 violations in 10 states). The accessibility score below rests on the measured targets, text sizes, overflow, fixed-bar overlap and hover-only checks.
- I missed one defect in round 4 that was already visible in my own numbers (legend popover under the fixed tab bar at 320x640 and landscape). It is listed as new item 2 below.

## 1. Verdict

**Overall 8.97 / 10, call it 9.0. Round 4 was 8.6. Every round-4 blocker is fixed or clearly improved, but Speed, Delight and Accessibility are each still under 9 (8.8), so the bar is met on average, not on every axis.**

What is now good, with evidence:
- **Run triage no longer collapses the page.** Page height only grows (1368 to 2548 px, never below 1368; round 4 went 1358 to 895 to 1827). At 60 ms after the tap the VERDICT skeleton is already at the top of the screen with a reserved block below it; the verdict text is there at 150 ms (`triage-375x812-T60.png`, `-T150.png`). Standard CLS 0.0002.
- **Paste-your-repo result no longer reflows on the happy path.** `axios/axios`: standard CLS 0.0004 to 0.052 across 375x812, 320x640 and 390x844 (round 4: 0.46 to 0.97). A full `https://github.com/axios/axios` URL also works (CLS 0.037, 2.4 s).
- **The autoplay now has a caption** that follows the playhead: "Before 13:12: nothing malicious yet", then "13:12 malware out · no advisory until 14:26" (from 1.9 s), then "14:26 first OSV record · 74 min late" (from 11.7 s). No horizontal clipping at 375, 320, 390.
- **The false empty state is fixed.** Prod only + search `zzzz` now says "No matching repos / No repo matches these filters." with a 102x44 "Clear filters" (`table-375x812-empty-prod-zzzz.png`).
- **The app's own share link now opens the thing you shared.** The Table's "Copy share link" on github/docs produces `...&repo=github/docs&open=fix`; in a fresh context the Fix drawer is on github/docs at 0.6 to 0.7 s.
- **Mark remediated keeps a one-row bar** (Copy JSON, "Remediated  Undo", More actions, all 44 px high); Undo is a 44x44 target (`drawer-375x812-03-remediated.png`).
- Ask answer still lands at y 28 to 57, above the Ask bar, at 375, 390, 320 and with the simulated keyboard. [E1] still opens its card at y 12 with the Cypher in view.
- 0 horizontal overflow, 0 text under 12 px, 0 targets under 44x44 outside sentences, 0 console errors in 4 viewports x 10 states.
- Production cold load on Fast 3G + 4x CPU: first paint 1.73 s, FCP/LCP/CTA 3.24 to 3.28 s, 10 requests, 474 KB, tap to verdict 2.46 s.

What keeps three axes under 9 (section 5): the paste dead-end path (yarn, pnpm, no lockfile) reflows hard (CLS 0.07 to 0.38), the tier legend is partly under the fixed tab bar at 320x640 and in landscape, and the in-sentence proof chips are still 35 to 43 px effective targets.
